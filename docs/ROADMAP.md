# PulseHive roadmap (what comes next)

Done: accounts, profiles and photos, posts and feed, likes, comments (with likes and replies), follows, Discover,
private messages, notifications, XP and levels (private standing), sharing, settings and privacy, admin dashboard,
reports and moderation, Terms and Privacy Policy, admin two-step login, logo and domain.

## Phase 13: Profile "About" (education and more)
- Education: add several schools (primary, secondary, college/university, other) with school name, years, and field of study.
- Work: add jobs (title, place, years), plus current city and hometown.
- Shown on the profile in an "About" section; each item can be edited, removed, and set to Public or Only me.
- Optional XP for completing your profile.

## Phase 14: Group chats (in Messages)
- Start a chat with several people; name it, add or remove members, leave it.
- Same live messaging, unread badges and notifications as one-to-one chats; the creator can manage members.

## Phase 15: Pages (for your brand or business)
- Create a Page (name, category, about, photo, cover, website and contact buttons).
- Pages post updates that followers see in their feed; people can follow a Page; owners and helpers can manage it.
- "Advertising" here means free Page posts that reach followers. Paid ads (payments, targeting, reports) would be a much bigger separate project.

## Phase 16: Community groups
- Create public or private groups with a name, description and rules.
- Join, leave, or request to join; members post inside the group; admins and moderators manage members and posts.
- A group feed, group search in Discover, group reports for moderation.

## Phase 17: The phone app
- An Expo/React Native app that uses the same database, so everything you built carries over.
